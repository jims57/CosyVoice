 Streaming MP3 chunks are designed for concatenation
Your audio_*.mp3 files are streaming chunks generated by a streaming MP3 encoder (like from real-time audio processing). These chunks have special properties:
Frame-aligned boundaries: Each chunk starts and ends at exact MP3 frame boundaries
No individual headers: Minimal or no ID3 tags/metadata per chunk
Consistent encoding: Same sample rate, bitrate, channel config across all chunks
Seamless design: Specifically created to be concatenated without gaps
